QUEUE WORKER SIZING (LITTLE'S LAW)
declare the SLA: "a job waits at most 60s before pickup" and derive the workers
workers = pending jobs ÷ SLA seconds ÷ jobs/s per worker
(needed throughput to drain the backlog in time, divided by what one worker can do)
STEADY STATE
100 pending · 60s SLA · 10 jobs/s each
(100 ÷ 60) ÷ 10 → 1 worker
SPIKE, 5s OF SLA LEFT
500 pending · oldest job 55s old · 8 jobs/s each
(500 ÷ 5) ÷ 8 = 13 × safety margin → 16 workers
supervisor numprocs=10 is a guess  ·  this recomputes every 5s, capped by host RAM/CPU
